Effective blood management is a critical component of modern healthcare systems, directly impacting patient outcomes, resource utilization, and operational efficiency. This analysis explores the lifecycle of blood—from donation and collection to testing, storage, distribution, and transfusion—through a data-driven lens. By modeling key entities such as donors, blood types, donation events, inventory levels, and transfusion records, we aim to uncover patterns and optimize processes that support timely and safe blood delivery.
The goal is to design a scalable relational database schema that captures the complexity of blood management while enabling robust analytics. This includes tracking donor eligibility, managing many-to-many relationships between donors and donation events, and analyzing compatibility matrices for transfusions. Through structured queries and schema refinement, we seek to support decision-making in areas such as inventory forecasting, donor outreach, and transfusion safety.
